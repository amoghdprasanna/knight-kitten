HOW TO PLAY

Arena (Story, Endless, Boss Rush, Daily): move with WASD / arrows / stick. J attack (3 hits = launcher), K jump (twice = double jump), L dash (hold to sprint). Jump + attack = ground pound. Climb rocks, bales and haystacks. Stop alarms reaching the cottage door.
Journey: hold jump to charge a higher leap, press again in the air to double jump, attack to slash. Rescue stray cats, collect fish, reach Grandma's.
Touch: left side = stick (Journey: jump), buttons on the right. Gamepad: A jump, X attack, B dash.
